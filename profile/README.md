## Craftosphere

Craft over chores. Well-made systems for small businesses, and engineering judgment for software teams.

Craftosphere is a software company in Budapest, Hungary. It builds well-made systems for small businesses and engineering judgment in software teams, so that people's time goes to their craft, not to chores.

### What Craftosphere does

- 🔧 For small businesses, Craftosphere starts with an evaluation and then builds the system that means less manual work. It judges which work is worth handing to a system, with what tool, and what to leave alone.
- 🥋 For software companies, Craftosphere offers three products. The Engineering Quality Scorecard shows where a team's judgment is, the Judgment Dojo builds it every week, and the Precision Sprint applies it with the team to one bounded problem. The work is hands-on: engineering engagements, technical coaching, and speaking.
- 🌊 Craftosphere uses [nWave](https://nwave.ai), the open-source AI-assisted development framework that enforces TDD and peer review, in its engagements.
